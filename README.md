# J A R V I S — A Quiet Intelligence

> A hypnotic, meditative 3D holographic screensaver inspired by J.A.R.V.I.S.'s golden consciousness matrix in *Marvel's Avengers: Age of Ultron* (2015).

---

## 1. Visual Anatomy (From the *Age of Ultron* Reference Frames)

Built directly from the 4 Stark Lab holographic projection frames in *Age of Ultron*:

1. **The Hollow Toroidal "Iris / Eye" Core:**
   Instead of a solid glowing orb, the center of J.A.R.V.I.S. is a **hollow dark aperture** ringed by 22 tightly bundled, shimmering golden filament loops and sweeping inner calligraphic crescents.
2. **Paired Radial Spoke Trusses & Traveling Synaptic Pulses:**
   28 twin-strut radial optical spokes bridge the inner iris aperture out to the concentric spherical shells, with warm bioluminescent pulses gliding inward and outward along the struts.
3. **58 Woven Hairline Spherical Shells & Stippled Tracks:**
   Hundreds of ultra-fine (`0.45px–1.1px`) dashed golden arcs, micro-tick comb ladders, and meridian ribbons counter-rotate at a slow, hypnotic, underwater-like cadence.
4. **Asymmetric Outer Terraced C-Ribbons & Circuit Comb Ladders:**
   Stepped circuit-board terraces, radial ladder teeth, and an equatorial astrolabe lobe extend along the left and lower-left periphery.
5. **Optical Bokeh Depth-of-Field:**
   2,200 3D golden nodes render as crisp `1px` champagne-gold pinpoints near the focal plane and soft, out-of-focus warm amber bokeh discs in the near foreground and far background.

## 2. Minimalist, Distraction-Free Screensaver UI

No noisy HUD boxes, no frantic millisecond counters, and no dashboard clutter:
- **Top-Left:** Subtle `J A R V I S` / `A QUIET INTELLIGENCE` wordmark.
- **Bottom-Center:** Calm, unboxed floating time (`8:42 PM`) and wide-tracked date (`TUESDAY, OCTOBER 20`).
- **Bottom-Right:** Four discreet circular icon buttons (`Pause`, `12H/24H Clock`, `Meditative Harmonic Drone`, `Fullscreen`) that automatically fade away—along with the mouse cursor—after 2.8 seconds of inactivity.
- **Lissajous OLED Burn-In Drift:** Imperceptibly precesses the 3D origin and typography along a slow figure-8 path ($\pm 22\text{px}$ X, $\pm 14\text{px}$ Y) to protect OLED panels during multi-hour runs.

## 3. Quick Start

- **Windows Fullscreen Kiosk Screensaver:** Double-click [`launch-screensaver.bat`](launch-screensaver.bat)
- **Browser:** Open [`index.html`](index.html) directly or run `npm start`
- **Run Pressure-Test & Audit Suite:** `npm test`

### Keyboard Shortcuts
- **`Space`** — Pause / Resume motion
- **`F`** or **Double-Click** — Toggle Fullscreen
- **`T`** — Toggle 12-Hour (`8:42 PM`) / 24-Hour (`20:42`) time
- **`A`** or **`M`** — Toggle meditative low-pass ambient harmonic drone
- **Pointer Drag** — Gently orbit the 3D hologram
